# What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that creates new content—such as text, images, code, audio, or videos—by learning patterns from large amounts of training data.

# What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making decisions.

# What is a Foundation Model?

A foundation model is a large, pretrained AI model trained on diverse and massive datasets. It serves as a general-purpose base that can be adapted to perform many downstream tasks such as question answering, code generation, translation, summarization, and content creation.

# What is the architecture of a Foundation Model?

A foundation model is built using the Transformer architecture. During training, large amounts of text are cleaned and tokenized. Tokens are converted into embeddings, and positional information is added so the model understands word order. The embeddings pass through multiple Transformer layers containing self-attention, multi-head attention, feed-forward networks, residual connections, and layer normalization. Finally, an output layer predicts the next token. By repeating this prediction process, the model generates complete responses.
